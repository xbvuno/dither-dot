algorithms
[01] DIFFUSION

FLOYD-STEINBERG

Classic 2-row error diffusion distributing quantization error with optimal 7/16, 3/16, 5/16, 1/16 weights.

[02] ORDERED

BAYER MATRIX

Structured 2×2, 4×4, and 8×8 spatial matrices creating signature cross-hatch retro textures.

[03] DIFFUSION

ATKINSON

Bill Atkinson's classic Macintosh formula diffusing 3/4 of error for punchy high-contrast monochrome art.

[04] DIFFUSION

SIERRA & SIERRA LITE

Balanced multi-row error distribution minimizing diagonal worm artifacts with smooth tonal transitions.

[05] DIFFUSION

BURKES & STUCKI

Fast and clean diffusion kernels tailored for sharp edge reproduction and detailed high-density output.

[06] QUANTIZATION

K-MEANS & MEDIAN-CUT

Color space clustering and octree quantization running in both RGB and perceptually uniform LAB space.
